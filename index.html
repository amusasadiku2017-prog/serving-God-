Do you know there are wonders in serving God?

Many people think serving God is boring or stressful. But those who truly serve Him know there is a wonder that the world cannot explain.

1. Joy that the world cannot give

When you serve God, you have peace inside even when life is rough outside.

Philippians 4:7 - "And the peace of God... will guard your hearts"

2. God fights your battles

When you put God first, you don't fight alone anymore. He takes over.

Exodus 14:14 - "The Lord will fight for you; you need only to be still."

3. Your life gains true meaning

Serving God gives you purpose. You are not just existing, you are living for eternity.

4. Open doors and divine favour

God surrounds those who serve Him with favour. Doors you could not open will open by grace.

Psalm 5:12 - "Surely, Lord, you bless the righteous; you surround them with your favor"

5. Eternal Reward

Nothing you do for God is wasted. Every prayer, every service, every sacrifice — God remembers.

Hebrews 6:10 - "God is not unjust; he will not forget your work"

Conclusion

Serving God is not about being perfect. It's about having a willing heart. The wonder starts the day you say, "Lord, here I am, use me."

Will you serve Him today?
